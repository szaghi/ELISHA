# SV-6: the spectral solver, isolated

| ID | Solver | Program | Leg | Figure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SV-6 (ADAM #81 P4) | `elisha_spectral_solver_object`, `isolated=.true.` | `elisha_sv_fft_mpi --isolated` | `sv6`, `slab` | yes |

## Case and scope

Isolated boundaries give the free-space potential, $\Phi \to 0$ at infinity, of the mass on the grid. The solver uses
Hockney and Eastwood's zero padding: the source sits in an array of $m \ge 2(N + n_{gc})$ cells per direction (the next
13-smooth size), zero outside the domain, and is convolved with the **lattice** Green's function of $L_h$,
$h^2 G(\mathbf n)$ (`elisha_lattice_green`: a table for $\max\lvert n_d\rvert \le 32$, the series
$G_0 + G_1 + G_2 + G_3$ beyond). The circular convolution equals the free-space one on every cell the solve returns,
ghosts included, and $\Phi$ satisfies the discrete Poisson equation, not just approximates it.

What the case catches:
- a kernel that is not the lattice Green's function of $L_h$ (a sampled continuum kernel, a bad seam between the
  table and the series): the residual of $L_h$ leaves the kernel's level;
- a padding too small for the offsets the solve returns: the error on the ghosts and the padded far field;
- a wrong mass normalisation or sign: the error outside the source, where the exact potential is $-GM/r$;
- a solver that accepts a configuration it cannot solve: isolated boundaries need 3-D, no periodic axis and cubic
  cells (the kernel is the unit lattice's).

## Setup

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Equation | $L_h\Phi = 4\pi G\rho$, $\Phi \to 0$ at infinity, $G = 1$ |
| Domain | the cube $[-1, 1]^3$, non-periodic |
| Grid | one root block of $8^3$ cells, 3 ghost layers, octree levels 1–3 ($16^3$ to $64^3$ cells) |
| Source 1, the $n = 1$ polytrope | $\rho = \sin(kr)/(kr)$, $k = \pi/a$, $a = 1/2$; exact $\Phi = -(4\pi/k^2)\sin(kr)/(kr) - M/a$ inside, $-M/r$ outside, $M = 4a^3/\pi$ |
| Source 2, the uniform sphere | $\rho = 1$ for $r < a$, cell averages of $8^3$ samples; exact $-2\pi(a^2 - r^2/3)$ inside, $-M/r$ outside, $M = 4\pi a^3/3$ |

**Asserted.** The residual $\max\lvert L_h\Phi - 4\pi\rho\rvert / \max\lvert 4\pi\rho\rvert$ over the interior at most
$10^{-10}$ (the kernel's own residual, about $10^{-13}$ per offset, summed over the source cells); the max error
against the exact potential over every cell and ghost, and its least-squares order over the ladder, at least 1.9 for
the polytrope and 1.2 for the uniform sphere (its surface cuts cells); no ghost unfilled; the rank-invariance check of
SV-2. Refusals: a periodic grid, cells $1/16\times2/16\times3/16$, a 2-D grid.

## Findings

- **The residual is the kernel's, not round-off.** It is $10^{-14}$ while every offset lies inside the table
  ($16^3$, $32^3$) and $2\cdot10^{-11}$ at $64^3$, where the offsets reach the series.
- **The polytrope converges at second order** (2.14, then 1.92: the kink of its density at the surface). **The
  uniform sphere converges irregularly** (0.60, then 2.10): its surface cuts cells, and the bound only asserts better
  than first order.
- Every solve is a symmetric linear map of the source (the kernel is even), which the exact exchange of the kinetic
  and gravitational energies of self-gravity needs.
